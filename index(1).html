About RIDS Clay Factory

RIDS — Reliable Indian Degradable Solutions

Rids Clay is a clay-product manufacturing factory focused on making and supplying traditional kulhads. The factory brings together people, मिट्टी and traditional craftsmanship to turn natural clay into useful everyday products.

10+ People Working Kulhad Manufacturing Supply to Customers
Our Core Focus

Kulhad — bringing मिट्टी back to everyday life.

Kulhad manufacturing is at the heart of the Rids Clay factory. Our team makes traditional clay kulhads and supplies them for everyday use.

The factory is currently supported by a team of 10 people working across the production process. Together, they turn prepared clay into kulhads that can be supplied to customers and businesses.

From preparing the clay and shaping the cups to drying and firing them, every stage reflects the connection between earth, human hands and Indian tradition.

Our Future Vision

Beyond kulhads — biodegradable household products.

Rids Clay is not intended to remain limited to kulhads. In the future, the factory aims to develop and manufacture a wider range of everyday household products designed to be biodegradable and more earth-conscious. The long-term vision is to bring practical, sustainable alternatives into daily life while keeping Indian craftsmanship and responsible use of natural materials at the centre.

“We don't just want to make things from earth. We want to build a deeper connection between people, nature and the choices we make every day.”

What is Rids Clay?

A brand with a purpose beyond a product.

Rids Clay is a clay-product manufacturing factory founded by Akash Yaduvanshi with a simple belief: the future can be modern without becoming disconnected from nature.

The initiative is inspired by one of humanity's oldest materials—clay. Earth, shaped by human hands, has been part of Indian life and culture for generations.

The Rids Clay factory aims to take that connection forward in a contemporary way: by creating awareness around natural materials, celebrating clay-based craft and exploring practical, responsible alternatives wherever possible.

Our motive

To make our relationship with Earth more conscious.

Rids Clay's mission is not simply about clay products. It is about encouraging a mindset in which progress and care for the planet can exist together.
